# {{title}}

> A focused investigation distilled to one page. The value is the distillation:
> what someone needs to know to proceed, not a log of what you tried.
> Keep this under roughly 150 lines. If it will not fit, the question was too
> broad and should be split.

## Question

The specific thing that was unknown, and what decision it blocks.

## Verdict

The answer, in two or three sentences, up front. A reader who stops here should
still be able to act.

## What you need to know

The minimum working knowledge — idioms, required incantations, the shape of a
working setup. Prefer a small example that runs over prose that describes.

## Traps

Things that cost time and would cost it again. The highest-value section in a
spike, and the one most often left out.
